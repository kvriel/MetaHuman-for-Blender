MetaHuman for Blender - macOS installer

Install
1. Quit Blender.
2. Double-click "Install MetaHuman for Blender.command".
3. A Terminal window lists the Blender versions on this Mac (4.2 or newer is needed).
   Press Return to install into all of them, or type the numbers of the ones you want.
4. Open Blender, press N in the 3D Viewport and choose the MetaHuman tab, or use the
   MetaHuman menu in the top bar.

Uninstall
Double-click "Uninstall MetaHuman for Blender.command" and pick the Blender versions.

If macOS won't open the installer
The installer is not signed by Apple, so macOS may say it "cannot be opened" or is from an
unidentified developer.
- macOS 14 and earlier: Control-click the file, choose Open, then click Open again.
- macOS 15 and later: try to open it once, then go to System Settings > Privacy & Security,
  scroll down and click Open Anyway.
- Or open Terminal, type "bash " (with a space), drag the .command file into the window and
  press Return.

Without the installer
In Blender: Edit > Preferences > Get Extensions, open the menu at the top right, choose
Install from Disk and pick the mfb-<version>.zip in this folder. You can also drag that zip
into the Blender window.
